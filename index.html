Kaiyue Chen

Hi, I'm Kaiyue, an undergraduate student in Mechatronics and Robotic Systems at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. I work on tactile-guided World Models for robotic manipulation. I am passionate about letting robots work in complex, real-world environments.

I am currently at Peking University, advised by Prof. Shanghang Zhang. Previously, I was a research intern at Sudo Robotics, where I focused on tactile sensing and VLA under the guidance of Prof. Rui Chen.

Current focus

Research

  1. 01

    Safer robotic grasping

    Using three-way decisions to defer unreliable actions under ambiguity, occlusion, and human-hand intrusion.

  2. 02

    Contact-rich manipulation

    Combining vision-based tactile sensing with single-arm and bimanual control for contact-rich tasks.

  3. 03

    Tactile-guided VLA

    Connecting frozen VLA backbones with tactile-conditioned decoders for high-frequency action correction.
